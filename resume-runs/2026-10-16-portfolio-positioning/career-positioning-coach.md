# Career Positioning Coach

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Senior Full Stack Engineer | Strongest direct overlap with the recorded web, API, cloud, database and delivery strengths. | End-to-end delivery, product judgment, quality and technical range. |

## Positioning statement — working draft

**English:** Senior Full-Stack Engineer focused on turning complex product and platform requirements into clear, durable software systems through hands-on delivery, pragmatic architecture, and technical leadership.

**Español:** Senior Full-Stack Engineer enfocado en transformar requisitos complejos de producto y plataforma en sistemas de software claros y sostenibles, combinando ejecución hands-on, arquitectura pragmática y liderazgo técnico.

## Deliberately not primary targets yet

- Engineering Manager: the current source record supports technical leadership, not people-management scope.
- Principal/Distinguished Engineer: possible long-term direction, but needs published evidence of organization-wide influence.
- Pure DevOps/SRE: cloud and delivery strengths are relevant, but the current profile is broader product and application engineering.
