# Product Vision

## Promise

Marcos Vissio's portfolio presents three connected strengths: **I Build** hands-on full stack systems, **I Design** architecture and system design, and **I Lead** technical collaboration and delivery.

## Audience and opportunities

Primary audiences are engineering managers, technical recruiters, CTOs, software architects, and international engineering teams. The portfolio supports applications for Senior Full Stack Engineer, Senior Software Engineer, Technical Lead, Software Architect, and—where the opportunity fits—Staff Engineer roles in international remote-first teams.

## Experience

The experience should feel modern, premium, direct, accessible, and credible. English is the default; Spanish is available throughout. Visitors should quickly understand the professional profile, explore evidence-backed work, and reach out or access a resume.

## Differentiator

The Architecture Lab will let visitors explore architecture diagrams and the reasoning behind technical decisions. It should privilege clarity and concrete learning over decorative interactivity.

## Content integrity

Use only verified professional information. Employment dates, titles, outcomes, metrics, certifications, project details, and contact details require owner confirmation before publication. Unverified information must be clearly marked in internal source material and excluded from public claims until verified.
