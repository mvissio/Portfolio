# Product Requirements

## MVP goal

Ship a credible, accessible, bilingual portfolio that communicates Marcos's verified profile and makes contact/resume access straightforward. Establish content and navigation patterns that can support the Architecture Lab later.

## In scope

- English-first landing page structured around Build, Design, and Lead.
- Professional profile and experience presentation using owner-verified facts only.
- Curated engineering-project entries with an explicit verification state.
- Contact and resume section; final contact details and resume asset are owner-provided.
- English/Spanish language selection and translated MVP content.
- Responsive, keyboard-accessible, performant presentation.
- A minimal Architecture Lab entry point or teaser only if it does not delay the core portfolio.

## Explicitly deferred

Interactive Architecture Lab diagrams, ADR explorer UI, leadership case-study expansion, engineering playground, AI assistant, CMS/backend, authentication, analytics, cloud infrastructure, and deployment automation.

## Acceptance criteria

- A visitor can understand the professional positioning, browse verified content, switch languages, and find contact/resume actions.
- No public text states unverified achievements, dates, titles, metrics, certifications, or claims as fact.
- Core routes are responsive and meet the project's accessibility baseline.
- The implementation is documented, testable, and runnable by a new contributor.

## Content inputs still needed

Owner verification is required for employment chronology and titles, role narratives, project permissions and descriptions, outcomes/metrics, resume file, contact channels, visual identity preferences, and Spanish copy review.
